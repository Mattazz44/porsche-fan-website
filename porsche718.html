Porsche 718 Spyder RS

The Open-Top Mid-Engine Flat-Six Finale

Class: High-Performance Open-Top Roadster

The Porsche 718 Spyder RS represents the mechanical pinnacle of Porsche's mid-engine roadster lineage. Serving as the open-top counterpart to the 718 Cayman GT4 RS, it pairs a lightweight chassis with the 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six derived from the 911 GT3 Cup racing program.

"With the intake ducts positioned directly behind your head, dropping the top turns every 9,000 rpm run into an unfiltered acoustic experience."

To keep mass low and preserve balance, the Spyder RS uses a manually operated two-piece lightweight canvas soft top weighing just 18.3 kg. Output flows strictly through a close-ratio 7-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung (PDK) transmission calibrated for rapid track and mountain-road downshifts.

Lightweight Aerodynamics & Tuning

Featuring a CFRP front hood, NACA brake cooling ducts, and a prominent rear ducktail spoiler, the bodywork balances front-to-rear stability without needing a towering wing. Spring and damper rates on Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) are tuned specifically for composed cornering over undulating public tarmac.
