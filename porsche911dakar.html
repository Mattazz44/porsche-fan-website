Porsche 911 Dakar

Desert Endurance Heritage Reborn for Gravel, Sand, and Tarmac

Class: All-Terrain Limited-Production Rally Sports Car

Capped at 2,500 numbered examples worldwide, the Type 992 Porsche 911 Dakar honors the modified 1984 911 Carrera 3.2 4x4 (Type 953) that claimed Porsche's first overall victory in the grueling Paris-Dakar Rally. Engineered in Weissach across gravel stages in Chateau de Lastours, snowfields in Arjeplog, and towering desert dunes in Dubai, it expands the 911 envelope far beyond paved circuits.

"The 911 Dakar proves that the rear-engine concept commands loose gravel and desert crests with the same poise it brings to the Nordschleife."

Standard ride height sits 50 millimeters higher than a 911 Carrera with sports suspension, while an electro-hydraulic lift system adds another 30 millimeters for clearing rocky obstacles at speeds up to 170 km/h. Bespoke Pirelli Scorpion All Terrain Plus tires feature reinforced dual-carcass sidewalls and 9-millimeter tread blocks without sacrificing dry-asphalt braking precision.

Rallye Drive Modes & Lightweight Hardware

Behind the rear axle sits the 480 PS 3.0-liter biturbo flat-six paired with an 8-speed PDK and rear-axle steering. Two dedicated modes calibrate all-wheel drive torque split: Rallye mode biases power rearward for loose gravel agility, while Offroad mode automatically engages maximum ground clearance for deep sand. A CFRP front hood borrowed from the 911 GT3, a fixed CFRP rear spoiler, stainless steel underbody bash plates, and red forged aluminum recovery hooks keep DIN curb weight down to 1,605 kg.
