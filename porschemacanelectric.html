Porsche Macan Turbo Electric

800-Volt PPE Architecture Bringing Sports-Car Agility to the All-Electric SUV

Class: All-Electric High-Performance Compact SUV

As Porsche's first model built on the 800-volt Premium Platform Electric (PPE), the second-generation Macan transitions into a dedicated high-voltage sports SUV. In flagship Macan Turbo Electric trim, dual latest-generation permanent-magnet synchronous motors unleash up to 639 PS (470 kW) and 1,130 Nm of Launch Control torque, sprinting from 0 to 100 km/h in 3.3 seconds.

"Electronically controlled Porsche Traction Management reacts five times faster than a conventional all-wheel-drive system, adjusting torque distribution within 10 milliseconds."

Drawing energy from an underbody 100 kWh lithium-ion battery pack (95 kWh usable), the 800-volt architecture supports up to 270 kW DC fast charging, replenishing state of charge from 10 to 80 percent in approximately 21 minutes. On 400-volt chargers, integrated bank charging splits the 800-volt battery into two virtual 400-volt packs in parallel without requiring an auxiliary high-voltage booster.

Active Aerodynamics & Rear-Biased Chassis

Porsche Active Aerodynamics (PAA) pairs an adaptive rear spoiler, active front cooling flaps, and a sealed flat underbody to achieve a 0.25 drag coefficient. Rear-axle steering (up to 5 degrees), two-valve PASM dampers, and an electronically controlled rear differential lock (PTV Plus) give the Macan Turbo Electric a rear-biased 48:52 static weight balance and sports-sedan turn-in precision.
