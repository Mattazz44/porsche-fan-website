Porsche 911 Turbo S

The Timeless Yardstick of All-Weather Supercars

Class: High-Performance Sports Car / Flagship Grand Tourer

Since its debut in 1963, the Porsche 911 has served as the architectural reference point for sports cars worldwide. Building on the 650 PS 992.1 generation (2020 to 2025), the 992.2 Turbo S adopts T-Hybrid electric exhaust-gas turbocharging to deliver 711 PS and 0 to 100 km/h acceleration in 2.5 seconds.

"The 911 is the only car that you can drive from an African safari to Le Mans, then to the theater and onto the streets of New York." Ferry Porsche

Positioned behind the rear axle, the twin-turbocharged boxer powertrain channels 800 Nm through an 8-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung (PDK) transmission and active all-wheel drive. Adaptive front cooling flaps and a multi-position rear wing balance drag and downforce across high-speed sweepers.

Engineering Evolution

A widened track pairs with Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) and electro-hydraulic roll stabilization to keep cornering loads flat. Armed with standard Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes (PCCB) and rear-axle steering, the 992 Turbo S delivers repeatable stability across alpine passes and circuit straights alike.
