Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 RS

Mid-Engine Precision Meets the 9,000 rpm GT3 Boxer

Class: Mid-Engine Track Coupe / Weissach Flagship

For the first time in the Cayman lineage, Porsche Motorsport granted the mid-engine 718 chassis the prestigious RS badge. Sitting directly behind the driver's shoulders is the dry-sump 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six from the 911 GT3 and 911 GT3 Cup, spinning cleanly to 9,000 rpm.

"Process air intakes replacing the rear quarter windows deliver direct induction acoustics right beside the occupants' ears."

Carbon-fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) front fenders and hood, lightweight glass, reduced sound insulation, and door pull loops bring DIN weight down to 1,415 kg. A swan-neck fixed rear wing derived from the 911 RSR, front wheel-arch louver vents, and an adjustable front diffuser generate up to 25 percent more downforce than the standard 718 Cayman GT4 in Performance mode.

Nurburgring Benchmark & Track Balance

With ball-joint suspension mounts, forged center-lock wheels, and a 30 mm lower ride height, the 718 Cayman GT4 RS lapped the 20.832 km Nurburgring Nordschleife in 7:09.300 (7:04.511 on the shorter 20.6 km benchmark), proving more than 23 seconds quicker than its non-RS sibling.
