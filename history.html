Born on Track

Scroll down the central motorsport axis from the 1948 356 Roadster in Gmund to endurance dominance at Le Mans, Paris-Dakar, and modern LMDh prototypes.

1948
356 "No. 1" Roadster
1948 | Gmund Origins

The 356 "No. 1" Roadster

On June 8, 1948, the Porsche 356 "No. 1" Roadster received general operating approval in Carinthia. Weighing just 585 kg with a mid-mounted 1,131 cc air-cooled flat-four, it established Porsche's enduring focus on power-to-weight efficiency over sheer displacement.

1970 - 1971
917K Kurzheck
1970 - 1971 | Flat-12 Legend

The 917K & First Overall Le Mans Throne

Powered by a deafening air-cooled Flat-12 and stabilized by its short-tail Kurzheck aerodynamics, the Porsche 917K secured Porsche's first outright victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1970 before repeating in 1971 with a distance record that stood for decades.

1982 - 1987
956 / 962 Group C
1982 - 1987 | Ground-Effect Era

The Golden Era of Group C

The Porsche 956 and its 962 successor introduced aluminum monocoque construction and ground-effect venturi tunnels to endurance racing. On May 28, 1983, Stefan Bellof lapped the 20.835 km Nurburgring Nordschleife in 6:11.13, a benchmark unbroken for 35 years.

1984 - 1986
953 & 959 AWD
1984 - 1986 | Desert Rally

Conquering the Paris-Dakar Desert

Through the 953 and the twin-turbo 959, Porsche validated electronically controlled all-wheel drive across thousands of kilometers of African desert dunes. That rally architecture evolved directly into the traction systems used across modern 911 Carrera 4 and Turbo models.

1998
911 GT1-98
1998 | Carbon Monocoque

911 GT1-98 & The 50th Anniversary Sweep

Porsche Motorsport's first full carbon-fiber monocoque prototype mated extreme low-drag bodywork to a water-cooled 3.2-liter twin-turbo flat-six, claiming a decisive 1-2 overall victory at the 1998 24 Hours of Le Mans.

2015 - 2018
919 Hybrid & Evo
2015 - 2018 | Hybrid Hat-Trick

919 Hybrid & The 5:19.546 Record

Winning Le Mans outright three consecutive years from 2015 through 2017, the 919 Hybrid platform pushed Porsche to 19 overall victories. Unrestricted as the 1,160+ hp 919 Hybrid Evo in 2018, Timo Bernhard set an outright 5:19.546 Nordschleife record.

2023 - 2026
963 LMDh / GTP
2023 - 2026 | Modern Hypercar

The 963 Hybrid Hypercar Era

Uniting twin-turbo V8 combustion with standardized LMDh hybrid boost, Porsche Penske Motorsport campaigned the 963 across WEC and IMSA, conquering the Rolex 24 at Daytona and capturing world endurance titles.
