Porsche 935 Tribute (2019)

Homage to the 1978 Moby Dick Le Mans Racer

Official Premiere: September 27, 2018, Rennsport Reunion, Laguna Seca, USA

Produced in a limited series of 77 track-only examples, the 2019 Porsche 935 Tribute is based on the Type 991.2 911 GT2 RS and its 700 PS (691 hp) 3.8-liter twin-turbocharged flat-six. Its elongated carbon-fiber composite bodywork pays direct tribute to the legendary 1978 Porsche 935/78 "Moby Dick" endurance racer.

"This car is a birthday present from Porsche Motorsport to fans all over the world."

Built as a pure circuit machine tipping the scales at approximately 1,380 kg, it comes fitted with a welded safety cage, carbon racing bucket seat, air-jack system, 7-speed PDK transmission, and onboard fire extinguisher. Exterior aerodynamic details echo historic Porsche endurance winners, from aerodisc wheel covers to 919 Hybrid rear LED wing endplates.

Race Results: Spa-Francorchamps Debut

During the July 2019 Porsche Motorsport GT2 Supersportscar Weekend at Spa-Francorchamps, Egidio Perfetti drove his 935 Tribute to victory in both 30-minute races against a field of 911 GT2 RS Clubsport entries.
