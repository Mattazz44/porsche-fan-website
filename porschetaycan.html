Porsche Taycan Turbo S

High-Voltage Performance Without Compromise

Class: All-Electric Four-Door Sports Sedan

Built on an 800-volt electrical architecture, the Porsche Taycan Turbo S delivers repeatable, back-to-back launches and balanced cornering grip. Its updated Performance Battery Plus holds 105 kWh gross (97 kWh usable) energy to sustain high-output driving.

"Repeatable thermal management and a two-speed rear axle allow the Taycan Turbo S to accelerate like a Le Mans prototype without fading."

Dual permanent-magnet synchronous motors unlock up to 700 kW (952 PS) under Launch Control, complemented by a Push-to-Pass function adding 70 kW for 10 seconds at highway speeds. A planetary 2-speed transmission on the rear axle pairs brisk first-gear launches with relaxed high-speed gearing.

800-Volt Charging & Chassis

Under optimal DC fast-charging conditions up to 320 kW, the battery replenishes from 10 to 80 percent state of charge in approximately 18 minutes. Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes (PCCB), rear-axle steering, and adaptive air suspension preserve Porsche steering precision across every mile.
