Porsche 911 RSR-19

The High-Revving King of GT Endurance Racing

Class: LM GTE / GTLM Category

To maximize underbody aerodynamic efficiency and clear space for a large rear diffuser, Porsche Motorsport shifted the flat-six ahead of the rear axle on the 911 RSR. Introduced for the 2019/2020 FIA WEC season, the 911 RSR-19 expanded displacement to 4,194 cc with side-exit exhausts ahead of the rear wheels to free additional diffuser volume.

"The unmuffled cry of the naturally aspirated flat-six spinning toward 9,000 rpm defined a modern era of GT endurance racing."

Powered by a naturally aspirated 4.2-liter flat-six paired with a paddle-operated six-speed sequential constant-mesh gearbox, the 911 RSR-19 prioritized linear throttle control, aerodynamic balance, and 24-hour mechanical reliability.

Race Results: Le Mans GTE-Pro

Building on Porsche's 2018 1-2 GTE-Pro victory at Le Mans, the 911 RSR-19 claimed the 2019/2020 and 2021 IMSA GTLM titles and closed the factory LM GTE-Pro era with an overall class victory for the #91 entry at the 2022 24 Hours of Le Mans.
