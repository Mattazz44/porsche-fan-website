Porsche 911 RSR

The High-Revving King of GT Endurance Racing

Class: LM GTE / GTLM Category

To maximize underbody aerodynamic efficiency and clear space for a large rear diffuser, Porsche Motorsport shifted the flat-six ahead of the rear axle on the 911 RSR. That mid-engine packaging gave the race car sharp turn-in balance and stable downforce over 24-hour endurance stints.

"The unmuffled cry of the naturally aspirated flat-six spinning toward 9,000 rpm defined a modern era of GT endurance racing."

Powered by a naturally aspirated 4.2-liter flat-six paired with a paddle-operated six-speed sequential gearbox, the 911 RSR prioritized linear throttle control and endurance reliability over forced induction.

Race Results: Le Mans GTE-Pro

At the 2018 24 Hours of Le Mans, Porsche factory entries scored a memorable 1-2 GTE-Pro finish in the "Pink Pig" and "Rothmans" tribute liveries. The 911 RSR concluded its factory GTE-Pro campaign with one final class victory at the 2022 24 Hours of Le Mans.
