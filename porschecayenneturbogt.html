Porsche Cayenne Turbo GT

Twin-Turbocharged V8 Flagship Engineered for the Nordschleife

Class: Ultra-High-Performance SUV Coupé

Built exclusively in the sleeker Coupé body style with a contoured carbon-fiber roof, the Porsche Cayenne Turbo GT represents the most track-focused internal-combustion SUV ever calibrated by Weissach. Piloted by Porsche test driver Lars Kern, it stopped the clocks at 7:38.925 around the full 20.832-kilometer Nürburgring Nordschleife, establishing an official SUV category benchmark.

"Through Pflanzgarten and Hatzenbach, the Cayenne Turbo GT rotates and settles like a sports coupe weighing half a ton less."

In updated E3 II specification, the 4.0-liter twin-scroll twin-turbo V8 incorporates upgraded crankshaft, connecting rod, piston, and timing-chain components to generate 659 PS and 850 Nm. Exhaust gases exit through a lightweight, center-mounted titanium sports exhaust system that deletes the center silencer for sharper acoustic character and reduced rear overhang mass.

GT-Specific Chassis & Aero Package

Ride height drops by up to 17 millimeters compared with the Cayenne Turbo Coupé, accompanied by stiffened three-chamber air suspension, water-cooled active transfer case (PTM), Porsche Dynamic Chassis Control (PDCC), rear-axle steering, and increased negative front camber (-0.45 degrees) wrapping bespoke 22-inch Pirelli P Zero Corsa tires. Standard Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes (PCCB) and a carbon-sideplate roof spoiler with a 25 mm larger adaptive Gurney lip add up to 40 kg of rear downforce at top speed.
