Porsche 919 Hybrid & Evo

Three Le Mans Crowns & The Fastest Lap in Nurburgring History

Class: FIA WEC LMP1-Hybrid (8 MJ Energy Class)

Created for Porsche's 2014 return to top-class endurance prototypes, the 919 Hybrid pioneered an 800-volt lithium-ion battery system paired with dual energy recovery: kinetic braking recovery on the front axle and an exhaust-gas turbine generator feeding off the compact 2.0-liter turbocharged V4.

"Unshackled from WEC fuel-flow restrictions as the 919 Hybrid Evo, Timo Bernhard averaged 233.8 km/h across the 20.832 km Nurburgring Nordschleife."

Operating in the top 8-megajoule hybrid class, the race-spec 919 Hybrid combined nearly 500 hp from its V4 engine with over 400 hp of electric front-axle boost. Its 800-volt battery architecture served as the direct technical blueprint for the production Porsche Taycan.

Le Mans Hat-Trick & Tribute Tour

The 919 Hybrid swept three consecutive 24 Hours of Le Mans overall victories (2015, 2016, and 2017) alongside three FIA WEC Manufacturers' and Drivers' World Championships, bringing Porsche's outright Le Mans tally to 19. In June 2018, the unrestricted 1,160+ hp 919 Hybrid Evo shattered Stefan Bellof's 35-year Nordschleife record with a 5:19.546 lap and a peak velocity of 369.4 km/h.
