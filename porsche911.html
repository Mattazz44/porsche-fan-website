Porsche 911 Turbo S

The Timeless Yardstick of All-Weather Supercars

Class: High-Performance Sports Car / Flagship Grand Tourer

Since its debut in 1963, the Porsche 911 has served as the architectural reference point for sports cars worldwide. In its 992-generation Turbo S specification, it pairs daily-driven composure with relentless all-wheel-drive acceleration while preserving the unmistakable rear-engine silhouette.

"The 911 is the only car that you can drive from an African safari to Le Mans, then to the theater and onto the streets of New York." Ferry Porsche

Positioned behind the rear axle sits a 3,745 cc twin-turbocharged boxer engine featuring variable turbine geometry. Channeling power through an 8-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung (PDK) transmission and active all-wheel drive, the Turbo S integrates adaptive front spoiler lips and a multi-position rear wing to balance downforce across high-speed sweepers.

Engineering Evolution

A widened front and rear track pairs with Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) and electro-hydraulic roll control to keep cornering loads flat. Armed with standard Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes (PCCB) and rear-axle steering, the 992 Turbo S delivers repeatable stability across alpine passes and circuit straights alike.
