Porsche 963

The Vanguard of Porsche's Modern Hybrid Prototype Era

Class: LMDh / GTP Hypercar Category

The Porsche 963 marks Porsche's return to top-tier international endurance prototype racing across both the FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC) and the IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship. Developed alongside chassis partner Multimatic and operated by Porsche Penske Motorsport, the 963 targets overall endurance crowns worldwide.

"The Porsche 963 bridges high-efficiency hybrid electrification with the enduring mechanical strength of Porsche's racing heritage."

Under its wind-tunnel-sculpted engine cover sits a 4.6-liter twin-turbocharged V8 derived from the RS Spyder and 918 Spyder lineage. Paired with the standardized LMDh hybrid system, the powertrain hits over 330 km/h on long endurance straights while meeting strict Balance of Performance energy curves.

Race Results: Daytona & World Titles

In the Rolex 24 at Daytona, the #7 factory Porsche Penske Motorsport entry delivered Porsche's first overall victory at Daytona since 2003. Across both WEC and IMSA seasons, the 963 has captured championship titles, pole positions, and endurance podiums across North America, Europe, and Asia.
