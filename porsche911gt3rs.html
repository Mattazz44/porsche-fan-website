Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992)

Active Aerodynamics & Motorsport DRS for the Road

Class: Road-Legal Track Sports Car / Weissach Aero Benchmark

Developed directly alongside the 911 GT3 R and 911 GT3 Cup programs in Weissach, the Type 992 Porsche 911 GT3 RS reimagines cooling and airflow management from the ground up. Replacing the traditional three-radiator nose with a single large angled center radiator frees lateral space inside the front bumper for continuously adjustable aerodynamic flaps.

"For the first time on a production Porsche, a Drag Reduction System flattens the wing elements at the push of a steering-wheel button to trim drag on straights."

The two-part swan-neck rear wing rises above the roofline and cooperates with active front diffuser elements to produce 409 kg of total downforce at 200 km/h and 860 kg at 285 km/h, triple the aerodynamic load of a standard 992 GT3. Under hard threshold braking, both front and rear wings automatically pitch to maximum angle as an airbrake.

9,000 rpm Boxer & Driver-Adjustable Chassis

Its 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six gains revised camshaft profiles and single-throttle intake valves, delivering 525 PS through a short-ratio 7-speed PDK gearbox. Four rotary steering-wheel dials let the driver independently adjust compression and rebound damping on both axles as well as differential locking values mid-lap.
