Porsche 99X Electric

High-Voltage Formula E World Championship Lab

Class: ABB FIA Formula E World Championship (Gen3 Evo)

Developed at Porsche's Weissach research center, the Porsche 99X Electric serves as an active rolling laboratory for high-voltage silicon-carbide inverters, energy-harvesting software, and electric powertrain efficiency. Porsche designs the rear electric motor, pulse inverter, brake-by-wire controls, rear suspension, and powertrain software completely in-house.

"Nearly half of the energy consumed during a Formula E race is harvested back under braking at up to 600 kW without hydraulic rear calipers."

In Gen3 Evo specification, the 99X Electric activates its standardized front powertrain unit alongside the Weissach rear motor during qualifying duels, standing starts, and Attack Mode, unlocking temporary all-wheel drive traction that catapults the 859 kg single-seater from 0 to 100 km/h in approximately 2.0 seconds.

Championship Results & Road Transfer

Pascal Wehrlein captured the 2023/2024 FIA Formula E Drivers' World Championship behind the wheel of the TAG Heuer Porsche 99X Electric. Thermal strategies and silicon-carbide inverter switching lessons from the 99X program transfer directly into the 800-volt Taycan Turbo S and electric Macan road architectures.
