Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992)

Single-Make Endurance & Supercup Benchmark

Class: Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup / Carrera Cup

Competing across the Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup and national Carrera Cup championships worldwide, the Type 992 911 GT3 Cup is the first cup race car built on the wide-body 992 turbo chassis. Compared with the 991.2 predecessor, its 3,996 cc flat-six produces 510 PS (503 hp) up to an 8,750 rpm rev limit, paired with fully electronic gearshift actuation and a double-wishbone front axle.

"The extra power and double-wishbone front end change everything from the moment you leave the pit lane."

Synthetic Fuel & Cockpit Ergonomics

Engineered from launch to run on synthetic eFuels, the 4.0-liter boxer lowers carbon emissions across international race weekends. Inside, a high-contrast 10.3-inch digital display and carbon-fiber open-top steering wheel keep pit-limiter, brake bias, and traction maps directly under the driver's thumbs. For the 2026 season, the evolved Type 992.2 911 Cup raises output to 520 PS.
