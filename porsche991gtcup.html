Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992)

Single-Make Endurance & Supercup Benchmark

Class: Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup / Carrera Cup

Competing across the Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup and national Carrera Cup championships worldwide, the 992-generation 911 GT3 Cup is the first cup race car based on the wide-body 992 chassis. Compared with the 991.2 predecessor, it introduces 510 PS output, an optimized intake manifold, fully electronic gearshift actuation, and a double-wishbone front axle.

"The extra power and double-wishbone front end change everything from the moment you leave the pit lane."

Synthetic Fuel & Cockpit Ergonomics

Designed from the outset to operate on synthetic eFuels, the 4.0-liter boxer engine significantly lowers CO2 emissions during race weekends. Inside, a high-contrast digital display and redesigned open-top steering wheel place pit-limiter, brake bias, and traction maps within thumb reach.
