Porsche Panamera Turbo E-Hybrid

Executive Comfort Paired With Twin-Turbo Hybrid Authority

Class: Luxury Sports Sedan / Executive Liftback

The third-generation Panamera blends long-distance four-door executive touring with sharp chassis response. Using hybrid architecture, the Turbo E-Hybrid delivers rapid low-end throttle feel while retaining smooth highway refinement.

"The Panamera proves that executive luxury and sports car heritage do not have to be mutually exclusive. It is a true four-door sports car."

At its core works a revised 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 operating alongside an oil-cooled electric motor integrated directly into the 8-speed PDK housing. Paired with the Porsche Active Ride electro-hydraulic suspension, individual wheel dampers actively counteract dive, squat, and body roll in milliseconds.

Grand Touring Dynamics

A driver-focused digital cockpit joins rear-axle steering, multi-piston brake calipers, and an extending multi-stage rear spoiler, allowing the Panamera to transition smoothly from quiet urban electric cruising to high-speed grand touring.
