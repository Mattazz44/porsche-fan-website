Porsche 911 S/T

Sixty Years of the 911 Distilled Into 1,380 Kilograms and Three Pedals

Class: Lightweight Purist Anniversary Sports Car

Created to commemorate the 60th anniversary of the 1963 Porsche 911 and strictly limited to 1,963 numbered units, the 911 S/T unites the 525 PS 4.0-liter high-revving engine from the 911 GT3 RS with a close-ratio 6-speed manual transmission for the first time in the 992 generation. Rather than chasing circuit lap records with towering wings, Weissach calibrated every gram for unfiltered B-road involvement.

"Shaving rotating mass from the flywheel and removing rear-axle steering gives the 911 S/T an immediate, mechanical dialogue through every gear change."

An exclusive lightweight clutch and single-mass flywheel trim 10.5 kg of rotational inertia, allowing the naturally aspirated flat-six to snap toward its 9,000 rpm redline with race-engine urgency. Carbon-fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) forms the front hood, roof, front fenders, doors, rear anti-roll bar, and shear panel, making the S/T the lightest Type 992 model ever built at 1,380 kg DIN.

Double-Wishbone Precision Without Wings

Borrowing the motorsport double-wishbone front axle from the GT3 while deliberately omitting rear-axle steering to save weight and sharpen mechanical feedback, the 911 S/T wears standard 20-inch front and 21-inch rear center-lock forged magnesium wheels alongside PCCB ceramic brakes. A discreet Gurney flap on the extending rear spoiler preserves high-speed stability up to 300 km/h while retaining a classic wingless silhouette.
